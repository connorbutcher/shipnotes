Orbit Tasks 2.0 brings a brand new calendar view, so you can see your whole month at a glance and plan around launches, reviews and time off.

Breaking changes

  • The v1 export format has been removed. Use the new CSV export in Settings → Data.

Added

  • Drag tasks onto a day to reschedule them
  • Week and month layouts
  • Subscribe to any project's calendar from Google Calendar or Outlook

Removed

  • The @me search shortcut, deprecated in 1.3.0

Security

  • Sign-in sessions now expire after 30 days of inactivity
